# Maya's New Home
*CEFR A1 · Story 1 of 2 · British English · ~131 words*
*CEFR A1 · British English · ~131 words*

Hello, I am Maya. I have got a new home. It is a small flat in London. My family is very happy here.

The flat has got three rooms. The living room is big and nice. There is a red table and four chairs. My father reads his book there.

This is the kitchen. It is small but clean. There is a white door and two windows. My mother makes tea and bread.

This is my room. There is a little bed under the window. My books are on the table. My cat sleeps on my bed.

My brother has got a room too. His room is next to my room. He is young but happy.

I love my new home. It is small but beautiful. We are a happy family.
