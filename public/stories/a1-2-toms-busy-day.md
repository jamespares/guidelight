# Tom's Busy Day
*CEFR A1 · Story 2 of 2 · American English · ~143 words*
*CEFR A1 · American English · ~143 words*

Tom is a schoolboy. He is ten years old. He lives with his mother and father. He has got a little sister too.

Tom always gets up at seven. He eats bread and drinks milk. Then he takes the bus to school.

School starts at eight thirty. Tom is never late. His teacher is very nice. Her name is Miss Green.

Tom likes reading and writing. He loves playing with his friends. He can run very fast. He can swim well too.

He usually eats lunch at school. He often eats an apple or an egg. Sometimes he drinks juice or water.

Tom goes home at four. He plays with his little sister. In the evening he reads a book.

Tom goes to bed at nine. He is tired but happy. Today is a good day.
