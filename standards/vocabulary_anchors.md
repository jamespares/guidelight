# CEFR A1–C2 LEVEL-ANCHORED VOCABULARY SOURCE MATERIAL
*Anchor lexis by level × part of speech. Tags: EVP = English Vocabulary Profile (Cambridge English Profile); KET = Cambridge A2 Key Vocabulary List; PET = B1 Preliminary Vocabulary List; O3000/O5000 = Oxford 3000/5000 CEFR-tagged; Waystage/Threshold/Vantage = Council of Europe inventories; EVP-kn = EVP-calibrated lexicographic knowledge (not individually list-verified).*

## Method note
EVP assigns levels per sense, not per word, from the Cambridge Learner Corpus (learner evidence). Verified sense-split exemplars: degree A2 (temperature) / B1 (qualification) / B2 (amount) / C2 (a degree of sth); toast A2 (bread) vs a toast C1; grass n A1 vs mud n B2; unfortunately A2 vs fortunately B1; unexpected B1 vs expected B2; in fact B1; a number of B2; idioms concentrate at C levels (high time C1, go hand in hand C1, fall short of C2).

## Calibration traps (diagnostic distractor design)
1. actually: core sense A2; surprise/contrast discourse use B1–B2. 2. in fact B1 (not A2). 3. sensible B1 vs sensitive B1 (false pair). 4. eventually B1 ≠ 'possibly'. 5. sympathetic B2 ≠ 'likeable'. 6. pretend B1. 7. demand B1 (PET) ≠ 'ask'. 8. realize 'become aware' B1 vs 'achieve' B2. 9. embarrassed B1 ≠ pregnant. 10. library A1 vs bookshop A2; chef B1 vs boss A2.
